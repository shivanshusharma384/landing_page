LIVE 1V1 TELEMETRY

Chase Arena

Enter Chase Mode using the @chase command. Add up to 3 LeetCode friends to the Race Grid and watch their progress bars fill up in real-time as you all solve problems. No more asking "did you finish it yet?".

FRIEND TRACKING

Activity Feed

Stuck on what to solve next? See exactly what the users you follow have recently solved. Find patterns, check difficulty ratings, and get curated recommendations straight from your friends' success.

OFFLINE FIRST

Instant Search

Search through all 3,000+ LeetCode problems instantly. Built on a synced IndexedDB layer, lookups are lightning fast and offline-ready without waiting for LeetCode's servers. Just hit Alt+L and type.
